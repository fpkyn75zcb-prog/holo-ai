How to evaluate

  1. Check whether lips match consonants, not just opening and closing.
  2. Look for natural blinking, head movement and expressions.
  3. Note how long it takes to generate a short video.

If it looks realistic but takes too long, we'll need a dedicated inference service for live HOLO conversations.
